Support

ManaZone is a local iOS game from Phoenix N Fox, LLC. Here is how to get help.

How to reach us

Email manazoneapp@proton.me. Include your device model, iOS version, and what you expected versus what happened.

Do not send Apple ID passwords or full purchase receipts. For Remove Ads, Restore from Settings on the Apple ID that bought it.

Restore Remove Ads

Remove Ads is a non-consumable App Store purchase. It does not change how the game plays — it skips the rewarded video on retry.

In the app, open Settings and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID that bought Remove Ads.

Privacy

Play is local. Ads, if you watch them, are optional rewarded videos from Google AdMob. See the Privacy Policy for details.
