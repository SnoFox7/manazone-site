Support

ManaZone is a local iOS game from Phoenix N Fox, LLC. Here is how to get help.

How to reach us

Email manazoneapp@proton.me. Include your device model, iOS version, and what you expected versus what happened.

Do not send Apple ID passwords. Heart timers are consumable, so there is nothing to restore.

No ads

ManaZone is meant to be handed to a child. An ad is easy to tap by accident, and that tap can leave the game for the App Store or a website. The game does not show ads, and it does not sell a way to remove them.

Privacy

Play is local. There is no ad network. See the Privacy Policy for details.
