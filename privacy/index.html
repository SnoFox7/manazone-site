Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 16, 2026

This policy describes how Phoenix N Fox, LLC handles information for the ManaZone iOS app.

Publisher and app

  • Publisher: Phoenix N Fox, LLC (David Sno)
  • App: ManaZone for iOS
  • Bundle identifier: com.dsno.manazone

Local play, no accounts

ManaZone is a single-player game. Progress is saved on your device. Phoenix N Fox, LLC does not create user accounts, does not require sign-in, and does not operate a game server that stores your save data.

Optional rewarded ads (Google AdMob)

After the free retry band, Results may offer Watch to retry. That optional rewarded video is served by Google AdMob. We do not show banner ads on the play field, and we do not show interstitial ads on death.

The first time you choose Watch to retry, iOS may show an App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can decline. The game still works. If you decline, ads may be less personalized.

Google may collect information such as:

  • Device identifier and advertising ID
  • Advertising data
  • Product interaction
  • Coarse location inferred from IP address
  • Crash and performance data

Phoenix N Fox, LLC does not sell that information. Google’s practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy and AdMob’s help on data collected by ads.

Remove Ads purchase

You can buy Remove Ads as a non-consumable in-app purchase through Apple StoreKit. Payment is handled by Apple. We do not receive your payment card details. With Remove Ads, Watch to retry becomes an instant retry and does not play a video.

Restore that purchase from Settings in the app (Restore purchases) if you reinstall or switch devices while signed in with the same Apple ID.

Children

ManaZone is a casual game with an age rating of 4+. It is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Contact

For privacy questions, email manazoneapp@proton.me.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top will change when we do.
