Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 27, 2026

This policy describes how Phoenix N Fox, LLC handles information for the ManaZone iOS app.

Publisher and app

  • Publisher: Phoenix N Fox, LLC (David Sno)
  • App: ManaZone for iOS
  • Bundle identifier: com.dsno.manazone

Local play, no accounts

ManaZone is a single-player game. Progress is saved on your device. Phoenix N Fox, LLC does not create user accounts, does not require sign-in, and does not operate a game server that stores your save data.

No ads

ManaZone does not show ads. People hand a phone to a child and let them play. An ad is easy to tap by accident, and that tap can open the App Store or a website. We do not want that.

The app does not use an advertising SDK, does not ask iOS for permission to track, and does not read the advertising identifier. There is no Remove Ads purchase, because there are no ads to remove.

Two optional purchases grant unlimited hearts for a limited time: Ruby for one hour, and Sapphire for 3 hours. They do not change the cut, the walls, or the heart. They are consumable, so there is nothing to restore. Child Mode is a one-time purchase in Settings. It keeps a child inside Mana Zone. It does not collect anything new.

Children

ManaZone is a casual game with an age rating of 4+. It is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Contact

For privacy questions, email manazoneapp@proton.me.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top will change when we do.
